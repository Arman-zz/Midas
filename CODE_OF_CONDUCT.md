# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the MIDAS community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through a contact method listed
on the [Arman-zz GitHub profile](https://github.com/Arman-zz). Reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
